Source: https://www.ensogroup.com/wp-content/themes/ensogroup/assets/images/enso-images/hr_manual.pdf

Downloaded from the hyperlink embedded on page 1 of the supplied assignment. All 79 pages were text-extracted. Printed page numbers match the PDF viewer pages cited below. These findings come from document inspection, not executed LLM answers; The subsequent application verification passed; see TEST_RESULTS.md and data/evaluation.json.

1. **Privilege Leave:** 12 days per calendar year for permanent employees. No leave is credited during probation. Reference: page 60, [G] Leave Policy, Scope & Eligibility and Privilege Leave table.
2. **Probation:** 3 months from joining for employees up to Senior Manager. Confirmation depends on review. Management may allow one extension no longer than the original period. Reference: page 42, [B] Probation Policy.
3. **Health-insurance premium:** Not found. No premium amount was located in the extracted manual. The final application run also returned exactly Not found.
4. **Current GST rate:** Not found. No GST rate was located in the extracted manual. The assistant must stay restricted to the manual.
5. **Maternity leave:** The manual states 90 days with pay, described there as “12 weeks(90days)”, for confirmed female staff who worked at least 80 days in the preceding 12 months; it states no more than “6 weeks(45days)” before expected delivery. Reference: page 62, [G] Leave Policy, Maternity Leave table. The manual's week/day equivalences are inconsistent; preserve its stated 90 days rather than silently converting the weeks. This reports the manual, not current statutory entitlement.
