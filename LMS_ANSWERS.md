# LMS answers — HR Policy Assistant

These answers are based on the actual application outputs, verified against the supplied manual. Printed page numbers match PDF viewer pages for these passages.

**Q1. Privilege Leave:**  
Answer: 12 days per calendar year for permanent employees. No leave is credited during probation.  
Reference: HR Policy Manual, page 60, [G] Leave Policy.

**Q2. Probation period:**  
Answer: 3 months from joining for employees up to Senior Manager. Confirmation depends on review; one extension, no longer than the original probation period, may be granted.  
Reference: HR Policy Manual, page 42, [B] Probation Policy.

**Q3. Health-insurance premium:**  
Answer: Not found

**Q4. Current GST rate:**  
Answer: Not found

**Q5. Maternity leave:**  
Answer: 90 days with pay, stated in the manual as “12 weeks (90 days).” The manual contains inconsistent week/day conversions. The eligibility passage specifies confirmed female staff who worked at least 80 days in the preceding 12 months.  
Reference: HR Policy Manual, page 62, [G] Leave Policy, Maternity Leave table.

The insurance premium and GST answers are not supported by the manual. This project reports the supplied document, not external/current policy or statutory rules.
